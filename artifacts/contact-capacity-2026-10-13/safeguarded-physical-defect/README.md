# Safeguarded physical defect proposal — experimental

The strict 32-step GPU qualification changes original captured bounds while keeping original matrices and loads fixed. Cached and fresh GPU paths require independent original physical admission at every step and zero native fallback. This is a bounded parameter sequence, not an animated jump or FPS benchmark.

Baseline 91 x 882 fails at step 6 with cached, cold and freshly sorted prior active-row hints. Native fallback succeeds. Exact original VQC1 captures and failure bits are in ../triple-cache-sequence-rejection/ and ../triple-cache-sequence-sorted-diagnostic/.

VOXY_HAIR_SAFEGUARDED_PHYSICAL_DEFECT=1 stores residual history per original owner and row. Successive failed active-row residuals with opposite signs propose a compensated interior midpoint of the two effective targets. Complete original inequality, complementarity and force-balance gates and the eight-trial limit remain unchanged. This is a proposal safeguard, not a convergence certificate. Disabled mode allocates no per-row history. The experiment is not enabled by default.

Current-source results:
- Full physics suite: 423 passed / 63 ignored both disabled and enabled. App solver: 5 regular checks pass.
- 91 x 882: 32 steps, 51 factor reuse attempts, both physical paths admitted, zero native fallback, measured response/reaction differences zero.
- 55 x 2142: 32 steps, 31 factor reuse attempts, both paths admitted, zero native fallback, measured differences zero.
- Directly captured isolated 91-row regression: default mode fails GPU admission; experimental mode passes it.
- 100 x 882 still fails at step 1 with and without the safeguard. Its initial prior seed list is empty, so its extra sorted-seed diagnostic is equivalent to a cold start, not a distinct ordering proof.

The 100-row exact-bound reproducer is derived from the failure JSON and the parent VQC1. Matrices, RHS, loads and columns are preserved; provenance is recorded separately. Isolated original-physics replay still fails and exports its last failed physical trial for analysis.

The attempt to freeze already-admitted rows did not fix the 100-row case and was removed; evidence is in ../rejected-freeze-admitted-rows/. The unresolved 100-row case prevents a general sequence-readiness claim. Full jump completion, production readiness and >160 FPS remain unproven. Older full-cycle processes use their frozen previous binaries.
