## Follow-up: the initial rejection was a checker false positive
A freshly factored cold solve and freshly factored warm solve on the same changed bounds both reported 1.2859667757741944e-14 with the old dot-then-subtract helper. Exact rational summation of the saved binary64 products and bounds gives 6.924504427624608e-15 for both, with zero violations. See fresh-factor-decoded.json and exact-residuals.json; audit_decoded_residuals.py reproduces the check without floating arithmetic. The initial experiment therefore does not establish a factor reuse defect. The qualification helper now accumulates the negative bound and FMA product errors in a floating expansion before rounding; a regression covers loss at bound cancellation. Factor reuse remains disabled pending a corrected replay and original physical admission.
