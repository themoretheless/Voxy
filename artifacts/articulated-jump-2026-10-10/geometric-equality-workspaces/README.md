# Experimental geometric equality workspace capacities

Test-only policy rounds new equality storage to the next power of two, bounded by cache/device limits. Logical payload bytes remain unchanged for upload/admission/readback. Existing completed jobs use best-fit lookup. A failed enlarged memory-budget reservation retries the original exact size without dropping cached jobs. Ordinary production keeps exact-size allocation.

Actual GPU strict-budget regression passed exact-size retry, oversized rejection without pool changes, full/tail logical-payload snapshots, quarantine=0 and explicit retirement. The same complete native-407 physical operator (174 rows, 14 systems, 1764 coordinates, tolerance 1e-14) passed original physical and CPU comparison, cold geometric replay, and seven warmed alternating paired complete-projection timings with separate baseline/candidate pools. All physical response and reaction bits match. Counts/timing are in summary.json. Warm candidate allocations fell to zero versus 31 each baseline projection; equality dispatch/submission counts remain unchanged. Timing is noisy under concurrent full-model load; a 1.02846x median alone does not establish a robust speedup. Keep experimental pending representative controlled timing and broader safety/full trajectory. No FPS claim.
