Isolated deformation reconstruction investigation, 2026-10-02

Not installed in the production rig. Production still uses the 46-joint rig.
Candidate uses 54 joints and conditioned independent bone-heat weights.
No object and no legacy PBD correction; five left-hand closure samples only.

A fixed skeletal rotation field, normalized inverse-squared edge weights,
soft skeletal position anchors, and pinned pure-bone cores form an SPD
least-squares edge reconstruction. Replaced unconverged Gauss-Seidel with
Jacobi-preconditioned conjugate gradients. Assemble right-hand side in f64:
f32 accumulation caused cancellation and unwanted rest displacement.
PCG restores open rest pose to float32 precision. Convergence alone does
not remove the thumb-web defect.

Permit thumb-web vertices to move even where their binding is pure.
Then alternate local proper-rotation fitting (quaternion torque iteration)
and global PCG solves, 20 local/global iterations.
Full-closure LEFT measurements: raw extra thumb-web crease 1.668771 rad,
minimum whole-hand triangle area ratio 0.0999753; corrected crease
0.523656 rad, minimum area ratio 0.5522114. Crease still fails the .30
acceptance threshold. These measurements do not establish both-hand,
121-sample, contact, intersection, performance or native-runtime quality.

Self-viewed finger-0-arap-1-minus.png. Smoothness improved but it is not
proof of a natural grasp. The image is an isolated no-object diagnostic.

Measurement mapping uses exact float32 source positions, not 1um rounded
keys; rounded keys had collapsed nearby vertices in older auxiliary audits.
Files are prototypes, not shipped changes. Runtime and source hashes for
the original metacarpal binding are in metacarpal-adaptation.txt.
